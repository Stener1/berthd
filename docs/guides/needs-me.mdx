title: Needs me
description: Everything that needs you, brought to you one piece at a time, so nothing depends on remembering to go and look.
---

**Needs me** in the sidebar is a queue. One piece of work fills the screen.
Answer it or close it and the next comes up. The header says how much is left
(`7 left, about 9 min`), and the number on the menu is always the length of the
list.

## The order

1. Pinned decisions
2. Other decisions
3. Pinned tasks for you
4. Other tasks for you
5. Results to read
6. The close-out deck, then suggested groups

Within each, what has gone longest without you looking comes first, so what
you've forgotten surfaces. A new pinned question becomes the next card. It never
takes the place of the one in front of you.

## What comes in

- Agents waiting on you. One that asks you to do something counts as a task;
  any other is a decision.
- Sessions that ended on a question or a block with no form. The card shows the
  agent's last message and a reply box.
- Results you haven't seen since they ended.
- Work nobody has looked at for 3 days, with nothing running, in the close-out
  deck.

Sessions parked until a reply or a later time stay out. So do sessions with a
prompt queued, unless a question on the agent's screen is holding it up.

## What you can do

| Action | Key | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A choice | `1` to `9` | Picks it. `Enter` submits a form. |
| Read | `E` | You've seen it. A question that still waits goes to the back of its pass. |
| Not now | `S` | Leaves until tomorrow or a date, then comes back by itself. |
| Waiting on | `W` | Says who or what it waits for, and when to check back. |
| Close | `D` | Ends its sessions. The transcripts stay, so you can resume, and the record stays too. |
| Pin | `P` | Puts this work, its repo or its project first, until you remove the pin. |
| Next and back | `J` and `K` | Moves through the queue without deciding. |
| Reply | `R` | Goes to the reply box. |
| Open | `Enter` | Opens the session, on a card with no form. |

Close-out cards have **Close**, **Park** and **Still needed** instead.

What you put off is listed under **Later**, with who it waits for, and **Bring
back** returns it early.

## Groups

Sessions on one piece of work show together as a tree, with the session that
started the others on top. A worker's question appears in its group and you
answer it directly. **Group with** and **Ungroup** only change how they're drawn:
sessions are never merged. A suggested group is a card with **Accept** and
**Reject**.

## Cards

Every card is drawn from one small format, with a kind, a title, a reason, blocks
and actions. An action is one of a fixed list of verbs that Shipyard maps to what
it does, so a card can't bring a URL or a command of its own for a button. Links
show their real host, and what an agent wrote is drawn as text.

Work records are kept on each box. See [`berthd work`](/reference/berthd).
